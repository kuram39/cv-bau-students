# Deep-research #2 — calibrate junior vs senior on one fair skills axis

**Run:** wf_09848fe4-efc · 109 agents · ~20 min · 2026-05-31
**Bottom line:** Skills-based, **criterion-referenced** scoring (every candidate vs a *fixed* target set, absolute continuum) is the principled, evidence-backed comparator. Tenure is a **weak** performance predictor — penalising juniors for fewer years is statistically indefensible. Keep "proven" coverage as one axis; model "potential" as a **separate** axis, not folded in.

## Key findings (high-confidence, 3-0)

1. **Criterion-referenced > norm-referenced.** Scoring against a fixed target set (criterion) gives higher inter-rater reliability than ranking within a cohort (z=1.37 vs 0.88, non-overlapping CIs; Ochsner 2023, Glaser 1963). → **validates our design**: score vs the recruiter's curated target set, don't rank juniors against peers.
2. **Years/tenure ≈ weak predictor.** Job-experience operational validity ~.16 (only ~.03 increment over general mental ability); overall experience-performance r~.27, and *pure time/tenure* is the weakest operationalization (Schmidt & Oh 2016; Quiñones 1995, N=25,911). Penalising a junior for fewer years = indefensible.
3. **HOW experience is measured beats years.** Amount/count (r≈.43) and task-level (r≈.41) measures vastly outpredict tenure. → **exactly our premise**: score demonstrated *tasks/skills* (thesis, projects, brigády), not years. A junior's task evidence can outpredict a senior's raw tenure.
4. **Credentials weak too.** Education years .10, T&E point methods .11, GPA .34 (<.01 increment). → skills-based > degree/credential proxies.
5. **Top methods work equally for entry-level & experienced** (GMA + structured interview/work-sample). So one skills axis is fair across cohorts.

## Calibration techniques for sparse junior evidence
- **Bayesian hierarchical / IRT shrinkage**: stabilize thin-evidence estimates by borrowing strength from similar candidates → junior's few data points don't swing wildly.
- **Score banding**: treat coverage scores within a measurement-error band as *equivalent* — don't over-read small gaps. (We already show a confidence band; reframe as bands, not false-precision %.)
- **Surface uncertainty** (conditional SE / bands) to the recruiter rather than a single false-precise %.

## Design recommendations (for us)
- **One criterion-referenced coverage axis** for *proven* skill (= current skill_fit). ✓ keep.
- **"Potential" as an explicit separate axis** (learning agility), NOT merged into the coverage %. → our `bridge_fit` "potenciál" is the right instinct; keep it beside, not inside. Validated.
- **Show confidence/bands**, avoid false precision. (Ties to our "spolehlivost" relabel.)
- Separate student/experienced columns are OK for *calibration display*, but the underlying axis (coverage of target set) is already the fair common comparator.

## Caveat (important)
- Classic Schmidt-Hunter validity magnitudes now considered **overstated by ~.10–.20** (range-restriction overcorrection, 2022+ re-analyses). The **rank order survives** (skills/work-samples > tenure/credentials), but don't quote absolute validities as gospel.
