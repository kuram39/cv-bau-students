## Flags / concerns

### F1 — `LLM_THINK_ENABLED` defaults OFF (thinking silently disabled)

**Status: behavioral regression worth reviewing**

PR #3 established selective thinking at exactly 2 interpretive sites
(`translate.py`, `reason.py`) — both call `llm.call_json(..., think=True)`.
PR #19 introduced `LLM_THINK_ENABLED = os.environ.get("CV_BAU_STUDENTS_THINK", "0") == "1"`
as a demo-speed toggle. Since the env var defaults to `"0"`, **thinking is
off in every normal run**, including the demo.

Impact: the capability translator and recruiter reasoning pass run at
extraction quality (Sonnet without thinking) rather than the interpretive
quality that motivated PR #3. For a demo with 6 CVs and ~40 LLM calls, the
latency cost of thinking is low enough that this tradeoff deserves re-examination.

Recommendation: flip the default to `"1"` OR remove the master switch and let
the two `think=True` call sites decide unconditionally (the original design).
At minimum, document the current off-by-default state clearly in CLAUDE.md.

## Current state summary

**Concept: intact and sharpening.** The platform correctly extracts CVs,
resolves skills into ESCO, scores against a job ad's curated skill set, and
surfaces the breakdown to a recruiter in a two-column (student vs experienced)
view. PRs #12–#20 reflect a disciplined progression: fix blocking bugs (#17,
#18, #19), harden infrastructure (#16), then simplify the product to its
clearest MVP form (#20).

**New direction: single-target focus** (PR #20). The platform now assumes one
target ad and scores all uploaded CVs against it. This is a pragmatic MVP
decision — it makes the demo readable and the metric interpretable (skill
coverage %). The 3-axis weighted model is effectively suspended; bridge_fit is
secondary and personal_fit is gone. The weights in config.py are dead code.

**No risks introduced.** The most significant architectural shifts (#16 Postgres,
#20 MVP simplification) are well-scoped and reversible. The main open risk is
`LLM_THINK_ENABLED=False` silently running the interpretive passes at reduced
quality (F1 above).
