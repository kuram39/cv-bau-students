## Alignment with core assignment

Core assignment: AI matching platform that processes CVs (extraction of
personal data, education, work experience, skills, languages) and matches
candidates to job postings — with specific focus on students /
fresh-graduates / career-changers compared fairly against experienced hires.

### All merged PRs: ON TARGET — zero drift detected

**Evidence & classifier improvements (#28, #30, #31, #33, #49)** are the
heart of the platform's student-fairness claim. The `doloženost` (evidence
strength) system replaces the useless LLM self-confidence score with a
source-type-grounded tier (work > thesis/project > hobby/claimed). Fixing
`work` → strong (#49) ensures experienced candidates score correctly, and
the career-changer vs experienced boundary (#30, #31) makes the recruiter's
candidate-type split reliable. These are core to the mission.

**Compliance additions (#37, #38, #51)** are mandatory for any deployed
hiring AI: GDPR Art. 22 (automated decision with counterfactual recourse),
EU AI Act Art. 14 (human oversight / override), bias-audit disclosure (NYC
LL144 direction). Not scope creep — the platform would be legally fragile
without them.

**Performance (#24, #25, #26, #27, #29)** addressed real blockers: the full
200k-row ESCO pull was unusable on Neon (10 s cold start), and Postgres
sequence desync blocked all new uploads. These are pre-conditions for a
working demo, not gold-plating.

**Documentation (#32, #43, #46, #47)** is a presentation artifact for a
platform pitched to recruiters and students — Czech-primary README (#46) is

## Concept assessment: intact, maturing, on track

The platform concept is fully implemented and demonstrably working:

1. **CV ingestion** — two-pass pipeline (generic → role-specific), .txt + .md
   upload, skills extracted into ESCO namespace.
2. **Fair comparison** — evidence tiers (`doloženost`) surface how a skill was
   demonstrated, not just claimed. Students are judged on project/thesis evidence;
   the explicit `student` / `career_changer` / `experienced` classifier enables
   the recruiter's column view.
3. **Matching** — skill coverage % against a recruiter-curated target set
   (not raw ESCO bulk). ISCO-grounded resolver ensures the target set is
   role-relevant. Bridge-fit estimates months-to-ready as a secondary growth signal.
4. **Recruiter transparency** — drill-in shows coverage breakdown, evidence
   per skill (🟢/🟡/⚪), counterfactual recourse (what skill to add to reach N%),
   AI reasoning, and the original CV text. Human override + decision log is live.
5. **Compliance** — Model Card (EU AI Act high-risk, GDPR Art. 13/22),
   demographic-blind prompts, counterfactual recourse, bias-audit by candidate type.

**No new directions that contradict the concept have been introduced.** The
single-target MVP focus (all CVs scored against one ad) remains the right
choice for a demo that needs to be readable by a live recruiter.
